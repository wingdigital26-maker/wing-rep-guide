How the CRM works

  1. 1

    A storm hits

    Each night we read the weather radar. We see where hail fell and how big it was.

  2. 2

    We check each house

    How old is the roof? Did it just get a new one? City records tell us. No record? It says Unknown.

  3. 3

    We rank the houses

    Big hail and an old roof go to the top. Each house shows why.

  4. 4

    The Today list

    Each morning it shows who to knock first. The route is on the crew's phones.

  5. 5

    The house page

    A photo from above, past storms and a 3D roof. Roof sizes are estimates.

  6. 6

    Homeowners say yes

    A page asks "Was my house hit?" They type their address. They say yes to hear from the roofer.

  7. 7

    Results

    It counts roof checks (inspections) booked and done. Not clicks.

When they push back

Say back what they said. Then ask a new question. Never argue. Tap a line to see the answer.

"How much is it?"

First month is $500. That covers the CRM, your new website and showing up on Google. Jack walks you through the rest. Does day work?

"Just send me info."

Sure. It makes more sense on your own area. 15 minutes, day at time?

"Referrals keep us busy."

That's great. What does a slow month look like?

Right. We set up in winter so you're ready for spring.

Makes sense. Let's get you both on with Jack. What time works?

"Who else uses this?"

Honest answer: we're signing our first roofers now. We work with one in DFW.

"What if it doesn't work?"

Jack goes over the terms on the demo. You see your own area first.

"Are you storm chasers?"

No. We don't roof or knock doors. We're a software and marketing company. We work for roofers.

"No time."

I get it. That's why it's 15 minutes. Mornings or afternoons?

"Not interested."

No problem. How do you get most of your jobs now? If it's still no, say thanks and move on.

Never say or do

  • Any price except the $500 first month. The rest is Jack's to explain.

  • "Free roof." "We pay your deductible." "We handle your claim." These break the law in Texas and Oklahoma.

  • "Exact size." Roof sizes are estimates.

  • "We'll text homeowners for you." Homeowners must say yes first.

  • Results we have not proven.

  • The name of our DFW roofer.

  • Don't call homeowners. Call business numbers only.

  • Don't call before 8am or after 8pm their time. No more than 3 calls a day to one number. If they say "don't call again", never call again.
